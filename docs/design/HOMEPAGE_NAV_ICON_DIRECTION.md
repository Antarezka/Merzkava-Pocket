# Merzkava Pocket — Homepage Navigation Icon Direction

**Status:** Selected visual direction; keep as a design reference  
**Date:** 2026-11-07  
**Scope:** Homepage bottom navigation icons only. This does not approve the complete toolbar/panel silhouette.

## Selected reference

The selected icon treatment is **Concept 03 — Arcane Fusion** from the Persona-inspired navigation preview.

### Keep these characteristics

- Individual icon tiles use a distinctive asymmetric rounded-cut silhouette: softly rounded on opposing corners, with sharper clipped corners.
- Thin Imperial Gold (#F0B10A) outline on the inactive tile.
- Deep Ink Black (#17191B) / dark green-black tile surface.
- Pale Silver-Blue (#DEE4F4) icon artwork for inactive items.
- Active item uses Electric Lime (#EFFE59) as its tile fill, with Ink Black icon artwork.
- Active tile may rise slightly and rotate subtly to create a selected/emblem-like feel.
- Keep the icon symbols clean, legible, and simple at small mobile sizes.

| Color | Hex | Use |

## Boundaries

- This selection is for the **icon tile style**, not approval of the full Arcane Fusion toolbar panel, central emblem, panel outline, or footer treatment.
- Keep the previously saved Homepage pull-cord/header baseline unchanged.
- Do not introduce Victorian clockwork ornament, antique wood panels, or heavy metallic decoration into these navigation icons.
- Treat Persona 4 / Persona 5 as high-level mood references only; do not copy their exact interface assets, logos, typography, or artwork.
